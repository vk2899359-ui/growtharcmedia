(AD CREATIVE & UGC VIDEO FAQ)

Questions buyers actually ask.

How much does ad creative production cost in India?

Creative runs either inside a performance retainer or as a standalone production retainer from roughly ₹1.5 lakh per month, scoped to monthly volume — a D2C brand testing seriously needs 15 to 30 new assets a month, not three. Per-batch pricing is available for launches and campaign bursts.

Does UGC outperform studio creative for Indian D2C brands?

Usually at the top of the funnel, where native, creator-shot video beats polished studio work on hook rate and cost per click. Studio and motion work still wins on consideration and brand recall. The honest answer is that it is decided by testing, not taste: across 450+ ad creatives produced we have seen a 5.2x CTR increase by letting the data choose.

What is your turnaround on a new creative batch?

Five to seven working days for a tested batch from brief to delivery, faster for iterations on a winning concept. Creative velocity is the real constraint on paid scale — an account stalls when it runs out of new angles long before it runs out of budget.

What creative formats do you produce?

Direct-response static and video ads, D2C UGC and creator video campaigns, motion graphics, brand reels, product and lifestyle shoots, conversion copywriting and PR outreach — built to the placement specs of Meta, Google, YouTube and TikTok rather than resized after the fact.

How is creative tied to performance?

Every asset ships with a hypothesis — the angle, the audience and the metric it is meant to move — and is read against spend, CTR, CPA and ROAS in the same reporting as the media. Creative that does not earn its impressions is retired, and the angle that does becomes the next batch’s starting point.
