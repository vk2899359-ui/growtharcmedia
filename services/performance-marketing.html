(PERFORMANCE MARKETING & PAID MEDIA FAQ)

Questions buyers actually ask.

What does a performance marketing agency in India charge?

Performance retainers at GrowthArc Media run from ₹1.5 lakh to ₹40 lakh+ per month (about $2,000 to $50,000+), priced on the ad spend under management, the number of channels and whether creative production runs inside the retainer. We do not take a percentage of spend alone, because that rewards spending rather than returning — the retainer is fixed and the target is ROAS.

How soon should we expect a profitable ROAS?

Days 1 to 21 are structure, tracking and the first creative tests. A stable, reportable ROAS normally lands between day 30 and day 45, and scale — moving budget into the channel that is already returning — begins around day 60 to day 90. Our blended average across accounts is 5.8x ROAS, on ₹15Cr+ of ad spend scaled profitably, with a 42% reduction in cost per acquisition.

What is the minimum monthly ad budget to start in India?

For a D2C brand we recommend at least ₹2 to ₹3 lakh per month of media on a single platform — below that there is not enough data for creative testing to converge, and you are optimising on noise. For lead generation in fintech, real estate or home services, ₹1.5 lakh upward is workable because the conversion events are fewer and higher-value.

Which paid channels do you buy?

Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram), Google Search and Shopping, Performance Max, YouTube, TikTok and LinkedIn, plus programmatic media buying — paired with conversion rate optimisation and A/B testing on the landing experience, and multi-touch attribution so the reporting is not platform-reported revenue alone.

Why does in-house engineering matter to paid media?

Because the ad is only half the transaction. A click that lands on a template page loading in four seconds loses the sale you just paid for. GrowthArc Media builds the landing experience in-house — 99/100 Google Lighthouse, under 0.8s load, +180% mobile conversion rate — so creative, media and the page are optimised as one funnel rather than negotiated between three vendors.
