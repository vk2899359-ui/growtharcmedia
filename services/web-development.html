(NEXT.JS WEB DEVELOPMENT FAQ)

Questions buyers actually ask.

What does a Next.js website or e-commerce build cost in India?

A conversion-focused marketing site typically runs ₹2.5 lakh to ₹8 lakh; a custom headless e-commerce or fintech platform runs ₹8 lakh to ₹40 lakh+, depending on integrations, catalogue complexity and compliance. We scope from the commercial requirement — what the site must convert — rather than a page count.

How long does a build take?

A focused marketing or landing site ships in 3 to 5 weeks. A full e-commerce or platform build runs 8 to 14 weeks including UI/UX prototyping, engineering, QA across devices and launch. Performance budgets are set at the start, not audited at the end.

Why Next.js rather than a Shopify or WordPress template?

Templates carry weight you did not ask for: third-party scripts, unused CSS and render-blocking assets that cost you mobile conversions on an Indian 4G connection. A custom Next.js or Vite build ships only what the page needs — 99/100 Google Lighthouse, under 0.8s average load, +180% mobile conversion rate increase. Headless Shopify is still an option where the commerce backend earns its place; the storefront is what we rebuild.

Do you handle Core Web Vitals and technical SEO?

Yes, as part of the build rather than as a later retainer: LCP, CLS and INP budgets, image and font strategy, server-rendered metadata, canonical and schema markup, sitemap and robots configuration, and the /llms.txt documentation AI answer engines read.

What happens after launch?

Conversion rate optimisation. The build is the baseline, not the finish: we run landing page tests, speed regressions and funnel fixes against the live revenue number, and because the same team buys the media, a drop in conversion rate gets diagnosed as a page problem or a traffic problem instead of being argued between vendors.
