(BRAND IDENTITY & PACKAGING FAQ)

Questions buyers actually ask.

What does a brand identity project cost in India?

A focused identity system — logomark, typography, colour, core applications and guidelines — runs ₹1.5 lakh to ₹6 lakh. A full programme adding retail packaging, UI kits and a full brand book runs ₹6 lakh to ₹20 lakh+. Scope follows the number of surfaces the brand has to live on.

What do we actually receive?

Market discovery and positioning input, a bespoke vector logomark with full lockup variants, typography and colour systems with the reasoning behind them, comprehensive brand guidelines, retail packaging artwork where relevant, and UI kits so the identity survives contact with a website and an ad account. 120+ identity systems engineered to date.

How long does an identity build take?

Four to eight weeks for a core identity system, 8 to 14 weeks where packaging and UI kits are in scope. Discovery is not skipped — an identity built without the market view in front of it is decoration.

Do you design retail and e-commerce packaging?

Yes — including FMCG and confectionery packaging engineered for shelf and for the unboxing video, which are different problems. Kabeer Confectionery and Kisaansay are both packaging-led programmes in our portfolio.

Does brand identity actually affect performance?

It affects the cost of attention. A coherent identity carried through ad creative, packaging and the storefront lifts recall, which lowers what you pay to be remembered — we measure a 3.8x brand recall lift across identity programmes. It is not a substitute for media, and we will not sell it as one.
