Questions buyers actually ask.

How much does SEO and digital marketing cost in India?

Organic and social retainers at GrowthArc Media start around ₹1.5 lakh per month and scale to ₹40 lakh+ for full-funnel programmes combining technical SEO, Generative Engine Optimization, content production, social management and influencer work. Pricing follows the volume of content and the technical depth required, not a per-keyword rate.

How long does SEO take to show results in India?

Technical fixes and on-page work can move rankings inside 4 to 8 weeks. Competitive commercial keywords — the ones that bring qualified buyers — typically take 4 to 6 months of consistent content and link acquisition. GEO visibility in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews often moves faster than classic rankings, because entity clarity and structured data matter more there than domain age.

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and do you offer it?

GEO is the practice of getting a brand cited accurately inside AI answer engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Gemini — rather than only ranked in classic search results. GrowthArc Media was one of the first Indian agencies to run it as a dedicated discipline: entity authority, structured schema, digital PR and LLM-ready documentation such as /llms.txt.

What does your social media management actually cover?

Full-funnel organic: content calendars, in-house creative and reels production, community management, influencer and creator relations, and organic-to-paid handover so the content that earns reach organically becomes the creative the media budget scales. Across accounts this has produced 6.8x organic and social impression reach.

What is your client retention rate?

94%. Retention is the honest proxy for whether an agency is returning money — brands do not renew a retainer that is not paying for itself. We report against net business revenue, not impressions or follower counts.
