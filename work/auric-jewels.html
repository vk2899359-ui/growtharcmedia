AURIC JEWELS

Branding • Digital Marketing • Performance

Elevating a fine jewelry brand through luxury visual identity and performance-driven customer acquisition channels.

(01 / ABOUT THE BRAND)

Timeless Luxury Meets Modern Craft

Auric Jewels specializes in fine handcrafted gold and gemstone jewelry designed for modern connoisseurs. The brand combines heritage craftsmanship with contemporary aesthetic appeal.

To scale in a competitive luxury landscape, Auric Jewels partnered with GrowthArc Media to establish a distinct brand identity system and launch high-converting digital marketing campaigns.

(02 / THE CHALLENGE)

Market Differentiation

Luxury jewelry consumers demand high aesthetic trust, flawless visual presentation, and seamless online-to-offline brand experience.

  • Standardizing brand visual guidelines across digital channels
  • Building high-intent customer acquisition funnels
  • Optimizing ad creatives for luxury buyer personas
(03 / STRATEGY & EXECUTION)

GrowthArc Scope

GrowthArc Media structured an integrated brand strategy focusing on premium digital storytelling, ad creative production, and targeted audience acquisition.

  • Refined luxury visual guidelines & photography direction
  • Multi-channel Meta & Google search ad campaign architecture
  • Custom landing page UI design focused on high inquiry rates
(04 / VISUAL GALLERY)

Brand Impact & Execution

The updated brand collateral and targeted campaign architecture positioned Auric Jewels as a premier choice for luxury jewelry buyers across key regional markets.
